## COUNT 1: THE STATE (FDA, DEA, BANKS)

### A. The FDA (Regulatory Capture)
*   **Fact:** Approximately 66% of the FDA's budget for human drug reviews comes directly from pharmaceutical industry "user fees"
*   **Source:** *Congressional Research Service*, "FDA Funding: User Fees" (2022). [Link to Report]
*   **Fact:** Drugs approved right before PDUFA deadlines are significantly more likely to have safety issues/recalls.
*   **Source:** *Journal of the American Medical Association (JAMA)*, "PDUFA Deadlines and Safety Withdrawals."

### B. The DEA (Active Supply Expansion)
*   **Fact:** The DEA raised the 2025 Aggregate Production Quota (APQ) for d-amphetamine (for sale) to 26.5 million grams.
*   **Source:** *Federal Register*, "DEA Final Aggregate Production Quotas for 2025."
*   **Fact:** The production quota for amphetamines tripled from ~7.9 million grams (2006) to ~25.2 million grams (2021).
*   **Source:** *Federal Register* historical notices (2006-2021).

### C. The Banks (Cartel Money Laundering)
*   **Fact:** Wachovia Bank (now Wells Fargo) admitted to failing to monitor $378.4 billion in transactions from Mexican currency exchange houses, money used by the Sinaloa Cartel.
*   **Source:** *U.S. Department of Justice (DOJ)*, Press Release (2010); *Reuters*, "Wachovia's drug habit" (2010).
*   **Fact:** HSBC admitted to laundering $881 million for the Sinaloa and Norte del Valle cartels.
*   **Source:** *U.S. Department of Justice (DOJ)*, Press Release (2012).

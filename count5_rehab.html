COUNT 5: THE BODY BROKERS

When the machine breaks you, the "Recovery Industrial Complex" is waiting to monetize the pieces.

PART A: THE FLORIDA SHUFFLE (Patient Brokering)

The Scam: "We care about your recovery."

The Reality: Many rehabs (especially private equity-owned) pay "recruiters" for patients with good insurance. The going rate can be thousands per head.

The Receipt: In 2020, the DOJ charged $845 Million in substance abuse treatment fraud. One patient was billed $600,000 in 6 months.

The Malice: Some brokers even *give drugs* to patients to trigger a relapse, so they can re-bill the insurance for another "stay."

PART B: THE URINE TEST SCAM

The Scam: "We need to ensure sobriety."

The Reality: A simple urine test costs about $5. Predatory rehabs bill insurance $5,000 or more per test. Multiply that by daily testing, and it's pure profit.

The Verdict: It's not about sobriety; it's about maximizing insurance payouts.

PART C: THE MENTAL HEALTH VACUUM

The Scam: "We provide mental health care."

The Reality: The US closed its state psychiatric hospitals. Beds dropped from 370,000 (1970) to 40,000 (2014). There's nowhere to go.

The Consequence: When P2P Meth induces psychosis, or when trauma breaks a former foster youth, they end up on the streets, in ERs, or in jail. The system creates the problem, then offers a for-profit "solution" that is rigged to fail.

THE DEMAND: A HUMAN LANDING ZONE

We need **Immediate Intake. Zero-Barrier Access.**

A state-funded Medical & Psychiatric Triage System. A place where anyone coming down, or seeking help, can walk in, no questions asked, no insurance needed.

A place that provides:

  • Medical Stabilization: Safely detox.
  • Psychiatric Triage: Assess mental health needs.
  • Human Connection: Develop a personalized, long-term plan.
  • Placement: A guaranteed next step – therapy, housing, job training.

This is not about profit; it's about humanity. It's cheaper to heal people than to warehouse them in jails, ERs, and on the streets.

Change. Not just payment.

COUNT 4: THE ENFORCER (INSURANCE)

Doctors prescribe the pills. But the Insurance Companies hold the pen.

PART A: THE PRICE GAP (The Trap)

The Scam: "We cover your healthcare."

The Receipt: A month of Opioids costs you a $10 Co-Pay. A month of Physical Therapy costs a $2,000 Deductible.

The Reality: This isn't a medical choice; it is Economic Coercion. The system funnels the working class into addiction because the poison is cheap and the cure is expensive.

PART B: THE RED TAPE (Prior Authorization)

The Scam: "Cost Containment."

The Receipt: If a doctor wants to order an MRI or specialized therapy, they have to fight the insurer for hours ("Prior Authorization"). If they want to prescribe Opioids? Instant approval.

The Reality: The system is designed to wear the doctor down. The path of least resistance is the prescription pad.

PART C: THE PROFIT OF CHRONICITY

The Scam: "We want you healthy."

The Reality: A cured patient stops paying premiums (or uses fewer services). A chronic patient on cheap medication is a steady, predictable revenue stream.

The Verdict: Addiction is a "manageable cost." A cure is an "unnecessary expense."

THE DEMAND

The people deserve the Number One Best Option, every time, for free.

Health is not a luxury good. Pain relief shouldn't come with a risk of death just because it fits the budget.

Universal Best Care. Nothing less.
